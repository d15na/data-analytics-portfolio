HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition patterns and explore HR data.

Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Visualization

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* Total Employees: 1,470
* Attrition Count: 237
* Attrition Rate: 16.12%
* Average Monthly Income: 6.50K

Analysis

* Employee attrition by department
* Employee attrition by job role
* Attrition by gender and age group
* Attrition by years at the company

Key Features

* Interactive charts
* Slicers and filters
* HR performance indicators

Dataset

IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance sample dataset.

Note: This project uses sample data for educational and portfolio purposes.
